# v0.7 — Real-data narrow (GSE280947 JUN)

## Objective

Check that redesigned engines remain directionally coherent on
replicated JUN CUT&RUN and compare PELT vs fused/WBS on one
chromosome.

## Methods

Genome-wide production: `analyze_continuous_matrix` 200 bp,
filter absolute 0.6, PELT penalty 24, `min_bins=3` (v0.6
baseline).

chr19 only: PELT vs fused lasso vs WBS on the same coarsened
cache. Fused/WBS: `raw_effect`, penalty 8, `min_width=1`,
CVRs with ≥8 bins, `infer_regions`.

Fixed-peak counts reused from v0.6 (IDR-union limma).

## Parameters

Contrast JNKi − DMSO. Cache `cache/gse280947_100bp_signal_matrix.rds`
coarsened ×2.

## Results

FACT: genome-wide PELT **2 GAIN / 688 LOSS**. This matches
v0.4.1–v0.6. `n=13027` in the comparison table is **n_tested**
(includes STABLE), not n DER.

FACT (chr19):

| engine | GAIN | LOSS | n (DER or tested) | median width | bp |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| pelt | 0 | 13 | 489 tested | 1000 | 545000 |
| fused_lasso | 0 | 18 | 18 DER | 200 | 4000 |
| wbs | 0 | 38 | 38 DER | 1800 | 79600 |

FACT: all three engines are LOSS-dominant on JUN. No
direction flip.

FACT: fused calls are 200 bp (one bin) and cover 4 kb vs
PELT’s 545 kb of tested / 13 LOSS regions. Different operating
point, not a ranked win.

FACT: peaks (reused) 2 / 1688. ESD remains less sensitive.

FACT: penalty 24 vs 16 bp Jaccard on chr19 tested regions =
**1.00** (489 vs 490).

FACT: leave-out JNKi_R2 Jaccard = **0** (489 vs 483 tested).
The LOO fit is 3-sample **descriptive**, not a like-for-like
replicated test.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: direction on JUN is stable across engines.
Sensitivity and width are not.

INFERENCE: LOO Jaccard 0 is primarily an inference-mode
change (2+2 limma vs 1+2 descriptive), not evidence that
chr19 PELT boundaries jitter under a fourth replicate.

UNRESOLVED: genome-wide fused/WBS was not run.

## Failures

csaw/THOR: no BAMs. Chromosome-restricted engine comparison.

## Interpretation

Gate I partial: parameter perturbation is stable; replicate
drop is not a valid replicated comparison. Direction (Gate /
H6) holds.

## Decision

Keep production JUN as PELT min3 / 0.6 / 200 bp. Fused is an
exploratory narrow overlay, not a replacement.

## Next action

Do not rank fused vs peaks vs PELT as a JUN winner.
