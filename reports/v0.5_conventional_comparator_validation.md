# v0.5 — Conventional comparator validation

## Objective

Validate the Layer-1 peak-limma comparator on GSE280947.

## Methods

Union of deposited IDR peaks (DMSO ∪ JNKi), same four JUN BigWigs,
`log2(x+0.01)`, limma `JNKi − DMSO`, FDR 0.05.

## Parameters

5,398 union peaks. Same samples/contrast as ESD.

## Results

FACT: 2 GAIN / 1,688 LOSS. Direction matches ESD and the expected
JNKi biology. Zero opposite-direction 1 bp overlaps with ESD DERs.

FACT: median peak width 620 bp vs ESD 800 bp.

FACT: provenance is GEO IDR files
`idr_dmso_jun.bed` (4,960) and `idr_jnki_jun.bed` (1,632).

## Uncertainty

IDR peaks are occupancy peaks, not differential calls. Using them as
*regions* for limma is the predeclared Layer-1 design.

## Failures

Layer 2 BAM/count: **NOT_AVAILABLE** (no JUN BAMs).

Dataset B hiddenDomains peaks were **not in the GEO TAR** (POST HOC
island substitute; see independent-dataset report).

## Interpretation

Gate B: **PASS** for Dataset A.

## Decision

Comparator is defensible and direction-correct.

## Next action

Use it as the primary benchmark, not as truth.
