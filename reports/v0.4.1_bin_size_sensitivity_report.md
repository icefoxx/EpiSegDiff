# v0.4.1 — Bin-size sensitivity

## Objective

Measure robustness of the GSE280947 JUN continuous assay at 100, 200,
and 500 bp. This is a robustness analysis, not a search for the bin
size that maximizes DER count.

## Methods

A linear-scale 100 bp mean matrix was cached
(`cache/gse280947_100bp_signal_matrix.rds`, 27,255,223 bins) and
coarsened by integer factors 2 and 5 **before** `log2(x + 0.01)`.
Filter `absolute 0.6`, Gaussian PELT penalty 24, `min_bins = 3`.
Strong DERs were predeclared as called regions with `|effect| >= 1`.

Minimum physical emitted width:

| bin_size | min_bins × bin | observed median DER width | observed min width |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 300 | 300 | 300 |
| 200 | 600 | 800 | 600 |
| 500 | 1500 | 1500 | 1500 |

## Parameters

`config/validation/v0.4.1_gse280947_reference.yaml`. Contrast
`JNKi - DMSO`. `summary_fun = mean`.

## Results

FACT: all three bin sizes completed genome-wide.

| bin | tested | GAIN | LOSS | median width | p90 | n_valid | runtime_s |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 | 22865 | 1 | 139 | 300 | 800 | 194118 | 115 |
| 200 | 13027 | 2 | 688 | 800 | 1400 | 91390 | 56 |
| 500 | 3663 | 2 | 581 | 1500 | 2500 | 32037 | 23 |

Concordance of **all overlapping tested segments** vs the 200 bp
reference (not only significant regions):

| alt | effect Pearson | sign concordance | DER bp-Jaccard | ref DER recovery | strong recovery |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 100 bp / 0.01 | 0.858 | 0.973 | 0.046 | 0.070 | 0.101 |
| 500 bp / 0.01 | 0.863 | 0.988 | 0.156 | 0.248 | 0.184 |

FACT: strong-region sign concordance is 1.0 at both 100 and 500 bp.
No widespread sign reversal.

FACT: LOSS remains the dominant called class at every bin size
(139–688 LOSS vs 1–2 GAIN).

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: low DER Jaccard across scales is expected when the
minimum physical width triples or halves and PELT is re-run. It is
not evidence that 200 bp is “correct” or that 100/500 bp are
implementation bugs.

INFERENCE: 100 bp tests more, smaller segments (22.9k vs 13.0k) and
calls fewer FDR≤0.05 regions. Power per segment falls as width
falls; that is a statistical scale effect.

Last chromosome tiles after coarsening are the mean of a full 100 bp
bin and a short remainder and are not identical to a native 200/500
Views summary of that last tile only.

## Failures

None of 100/200/500 failed.

## Interpretation

Bin size is **dataset-dependent**. Effect signs on matched segments
are stable; called-interval sets are not. Width distributions cannot
be compared without the 300/600/1500 floor.

## Limitations

50 bp was optional and was not run. `max`/`area` were not gridded.

## Decision

Gate B: **PASS**.

## Next action

Keep 200 bp as the GSE280947 profile only. Do not promote it to a
universal BigWig default.
