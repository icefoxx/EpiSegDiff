# v0.4.1 — Limma small-variance diagnostics

## Objective

Characterize limma zero/small-variance warnings without suppressing
them, and classify influence on the GSE280947 continuous assay.

## Methods

`limma_variance_diagnostics()` on the 200 bp coarsened matrix after
`log2(x + 0.01)`:

- per-bin sample variance (vectorized)
- tiny-variance flag: `var < 1e-8`
- linear-signal of tiny vs non-tiny bins
- default `eBayes` vs production `eBayes(trend=TRUE, robust=TRUE)`
- warnings captured only inside the diagnostic (not globally)

Production `estimate_continuous_effect()` is unchanged.

QC replicate scatter no longer uses hexbin/`smoothScatter`. It is an
ordinary subsampled scatter in `plot_continuous_qc()`.

## Parameters

13,627,618 bins × 4 samples. Contrast `JNKi - DMSO`.

## Results

FACT:

- `n_tiny_var = 1,328,741` (9.75% of bins)
- median variance = 0.899; 1st percentile = 0
- median linear signal of tiny-var bins = **0**
- median linear signal of other bins = 0.133
- captured warnings (2):
  - `Zero sample variances detected, have been offset away from zero`
  - `1328741 very small variances detected, have been offset away from zero`
- Pearson correlation of default vs robust+trend effects = **1**
- median |effect| difference default vs robust = **0**

Bin-level FDR enrichment columns are NA (per-bin FDR is not stored
on the 13.6M-row matrix). Classification therefore uses variance,
signal, and the default-vs-robust comparison.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: tiny-variance bins are uncovered / near-zero signal. They
are the background that `absolute 0.6` removes before PELT. They are
not the 690 called DERs.

INFERENCE: default and robust+trend effects agree to numerical
identity on this 4-sample design, so the warning is not moving the
point estimates.

## Failures

None in the diagnostic itself. QC hexbin warning is gone because
hexbin is no longer used.

## Interpretation

Classification: **harmless / expected**.

Not potentially influential: tiny-var fraction is 9.8% (not a
majority), and those bins have zero median signal. Not materially
influential: no evidence they dominate called DERs, and robust
eBayes does not change effects.

## Limitations

Diagnostic eBayes is extra compute and does not change production
calls. Significance enrichment among tiny-var bins was not attached
because bin-level FDR is not persisted.

## Decision

Gate E: **PASS**.

## Next action

Leave production eBayes as robust+trend. Do not globally suppress
the warning.
