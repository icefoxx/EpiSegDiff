# v0.7 — Real-data broad (GSE110378 H3K27me3)

## Objective

Confirm GAIN-dominant Kdm6b-KO H3K27me3 behavior is retained
and inspect fused lasso on chr19.

## Methods

Genome-wide PELT and quantile-island conventional calls reused
from v0.5 (`quantile=0.75`, 200 bp, penalty 24, `min_bins=3`).

chr19: PELT vs fused on `cache/gse110378_200bp_signal_matrix.rds`.

## Parameters

Contrast KO − Control. Expected direction: H3K27me3 **GAIN**.

## Results

FACT: genome-wide ESD **4411 GAIN / 2165 LOSS** (median 1400 bp).
Islands POST_HOC: 886 / 360 (median 8400 bp). Both
GAIN-dominant.

FACT (chr19): PELT 340 GAIN / 121 LOSS (GAIN-dominant, matches
genome sign). Fused lasso 4 GAIN / 12 LOSS, median 200 bp,
16.4 kb total — **sign-reversed and tiny**.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: fused lasso + `raw_effect` + `infer_regions` is
**not** interchangeable with production PELT on this broad
mark. The chr19 fused call set is too small and
LOSS-leaning to treat as a broad-domain engine.

SPECULATION: TV λ and the MAD call/infer threshold over-shrink
long weak plateaus typical of H3K27me3.

## Failures

No hiddenDomains peaks in the GEO TAR (same as v0.5). No BAM
csaw/THOR. No new genome-wide fused run.

## Interpretation

Broad-domain production remains PELT. Fused must not replace
it on histone domains.

## Decision

Retain v0.5/v0.6 H3K27me3 production calls.

## Next action

Do not use fused lasso as the broad default.
