# v0.2.2 — Level I independence

**Objective.** Confirm GAIN/LOSS region calls do not depend on
topology.

**Methods.** `run_episegdiff()` twice on the same simulated GAIN/LOSS
cohorts with `topology.enabled = TRUE` vs `FALSE`. Compare
`region_id`, `chrom`, `start`, `end`, `logFC`, `pvalue`, `FDR`.

**Parameters.** 8 genomes (alternating GAIN/LOSS), 160 bins, 3 vs 3,
TMM, penalty 8. Table:
`benchmark/simulation/level1_independence_test.tsv`.

**Results.**

FACT: All 8 pairs have `intervals_identical = TRUE` and
`stats_identical = TRUE`.

FACT: With topology off, `n_events = 0` always. With topology on,
event rows may exist. Tested-region tables are unchanged.

INFERENCE: Level I is the recount + edgeR QL table. Topology is
strictly downstream. No hidden coupling remains.

**Failures.** None.

**Interpretation.** A real-data pilot can publish Level I with
topology disabled or ignored.

**Limitations.** In-memory simulations only.

**Decision: PASS**

**Next action.** Default `topology.enabled = true` for completeness;
pilots may set it false.
