# v0.6 — Final methodological acceptance

## Objective

Ask whether change-point adaptive domains have a reproducible
advantage over peaks and window aggregation, and whether
region-level inference is calibrated.

## Methods

Predeclared plan. POST_HOC: primary synthetic nrep **10** not 50.
Layer-1 = same BigWig+limma. BAM-csaw/THOR NOT_AVAILABLE.

## Parameters

Dataset A JUN 0.6; Dataset B H3K27me3 quantile 0.75. Synthetic
penalty 8, min_bins=3, filter=none.

## Results — required questions

1. **Niche vs peaks/csaw/THOR?** ESD is a **domain reconstructor
   on continuous signal**, less sensitive than IDR-peak limma,
   more sensitive than per-bin FDR, worse F1 than window-merge on
   block toys, better as planted width grows. csaw/THOR untested
   on real BAM.
2. **Region definition vs model?** ESD vs peaks vs windows share
   limma+log2. csaw/THOR would confound model+regions.
3. **Fair comparators?** Continuous ones yes. BAM methods
   NOT_AVAILABLE, not faked.
4. **Lowest synthetic boundary error?** **Window-aggregate**
   (median 200 bp vs ESD 800 bp).
5. **Width / effect / noise?** ESD F1 ≈ 0 at 200–500 bp, 0.74 at
   5 kb, 0.88 at 50 kb. Direction accuracy median 1.
6. **Gradual boundaries?** Not a separate completed table
   (UNRESOLVED / not fully run).
7. **Who fragments broad events?** Windows and ESD both had
   one-call fraction ~1 on planted blocks. Real B: islands wider
   than ESD by construction.
8. **Who merges neighbors?** Same-dir gap 0: ESD 0.9 vs window
   0.3 mean merge count. Opposite: ESD merge 0.
9. **Opposite neighbors separable?** Yes; dir acc 1.
10. **Matched-call secondary?** NOT_RUN as a formal cut
    (SECONDARY_MATCHED_CALL table not produced).
11. **Global null false DERs?** ESD mean 0.99 calls, P(any)=0.36
    per 200 kb (filter=none). Window ~0.
12. **Empirical vs nominal 0.01/0.05/0.10?** P(any ESD DER)
    0.30 / 0.36 / 0.48.
13. **Label permutations?** 688 LOSS → 0 and 0. Supports
    filtered-profile calibration.
14. **Selection bias detectable?** Synthetic none inflation vs
    window yes. Split diagnostic not like-for-like.
15. **Split-sample alter conclusions?** Unresolved (split counted
    raw segments, not FDR DERs).
16. **GSE280947?** ESD 2/688 vs peaks 2/1688; sign 0.984;
    csaw/THOR NA.
17. **Broad mark?** ESD 4411/2165 GAIN-dominant; islands
    886/360 also GAIN-dominant.
18. **Fragmentation without merging?** Synthetic opposite events
    not merged. Broad real-data “win” not clean.
19. **Class C mix?** C1 1.4%, C2 12.7%, C3 1.9%, **C4 84%**.
20. **C4 motif?** 34% vs background 25% vs shared 67%. Weak.
21. **Promoter after controls?** Yes: OR 4.8 vs A, 10–12 vs
    shuffle; C4 68% promoter.
22. **Promoter biological or geometric?** **Not width-geometric.**
    Unresolved biology vs TSS-biased calling.
23. **Across geometries?** Direction yes (JUN LOSS, H3K27me3
    GAIN). Sensitivity patterns differ.
24. **Compute?** Synthetic+cal 838 s; no BAM pipelines.
25. **Claims matrix:** see below.
26. **Falsifiers?** Opposite-direction real overlap; permutation
    recovering ~688 LOSS; C4 motif = background after better
    matching; window F1 remaining higher on *broad gradual*
    events if those were run.
27. **Alternatives?** min_bins floor explains 200 bp failure;
    C4 = missed weak promoters; synthetic inflation = filter=none
    z-score PELT.
28. **Decision?** **CONDITIONAL PASS**.
29. **Ready for manuscript-focused validation?** **Not yet** for
    a superiority paper. Ready for a **methods+calibration**
    preprint that states the tradeoffs if BAM-csaw is added.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: ESD’s niche is **wide-domain reconstruction under a
min-width constraint**, not general sensitivity or calibrated
unfiltered FDR.

## Failures

- Real BAM csaw/THOR NOT_AVAILABLE
- nrep 10 POST_HOC
- SECONDARY_MATCHED_CALL NOT_RUN
- Gradual-boundary table incomplete

## Interpretation

A valid outcome is **not winning**. Window-aggregation is the
better toy reconstructor; peaks are the more sensitive real JUN
caller; ESD is distinct, direction-correct, and mostly peak-distal
in its unique set, with modest motif and strong promoter
enrichment that is not a width artifact.

## Limitations

n=4; no BAM layer; unfiltered synthetic ≠ production filter.
