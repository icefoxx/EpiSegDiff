# v0.4.2 Phase 1 — Scalability profile

## Objective

Explain why v0.4.1 `filter=none` did not finish, using actual mm10
chromosomes at 200 bp on the GSE280947 z-score track.

## Methods

Coarsened 100 bp cache → 200 bp. Score is the frozen replicated
z-score `effect / max(|se|, 1e-6)`. Engines timed: current
`pelt_gaussian_cpp` and the new `fpop_gaussian_cpp`. PELT on
chromosomes with n>80,000 was skipped in the first pass to avoid
repeating the v0.4.1 hang; prefixes of chr1 were used instead.

## Parameters

penalty 24, `min_bins = 3`, filter none (all bins valid).

## Results

FACT: `segment_track` already splits by chromosome and genuine CVRs.
The genome is **not** concatenated into one PELT series. v0.4.1 still
built a 13.6M-row track and walked every chromosome inside one
R process without checkpoints.

FACT: on unfiltered z-scores PELT pruning fails. Candidate set size:

| series | n | PELT max\|R\| | PELT sec | PELT segments | FPOP sec |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| chr1 prefix | 5,000 | 5,000 | 0.57 | 1 | 0.06 |
| chr1 prefix | 20,000 | 15,202 | 5.13 | 12 | 0.20 |
| chr1 prefix | 50,000 | 15,202 | 5.93 | 74 | 0.67 |
| chr1 prefix | 100,000 | 15,202 | 6.97 | 258 | — |
| chr1 prefix | 200,000 | 15,202 | 9.87 | 513 | — |
| chr1 prefix | 300,000 | 15,202 | 12.3 | 808 | — |
| chr19 full | 307,158 | (not run in pass 1) | — | — | 4.69 |
| chr9 full | 622,976 | (not run) | — | — | 9.27 |
| chr1 full | 977,360 | (not run) | — | — | 14.4 |

FACT: PELT time on prefixes is roughly linear once \|R\| saturates
near 15,202. Extrapolating, chr1 (~977k) is on the order of 30–40 s
in C++, not an asymptotic deadlock.

INFERENCE: the v0.4.1 kill after >30 min was the combination of
(1) genome-wide limma + a 13.6M `data.frame` still live,
(2) PELT candidate-set copies allocated every time step,
(3) no checkpoint, so a long multi-chromosome walk looked hung,
(4) `setTimeLimit` cannot interrupt C++.

FACT: near-zero bins produce huge \|z\| because `se` is floored at
`1e-6`. That noisy series is why \|R\| stays ~10^4 instead of O(1).

FACT: a first-cut Gaussian FPOP finishes large chromosomes in
seconds, but on the 20k prefix its reported cost (1367) does not
match the RSS of its own segmentation (7998) or PELT (7059).
**FPOP is not used for the official `filter=none` run.**

## Uncertainty

UNRESOLVED until the full chrom-wise PELT run: wall-clock and peak
RSS for all 21 chromosomes plus region-level limma.

## Failures

None in the profile script. PELT on n>80k was intentionally
NOT_RUN in the first table, then prefixes up to 300k completed.

## Interpretation

The failure mode is **poor PELT pruning + monolithic process
lifetime**, not “chromosomes were concatenated.” Closure is
chromosome-wise dispatch, no per-step R allocations, checkpoints,
and (if needed) workers. Statistical score/penalty are unchanged.

## Limitations

Profile used one machine. Peak RSS during profiling (~6–9 GB)
includes the genome-wide transformed matrix used to build z.

## Decision

Proceed to chrom-wise PELT (legacy-equivalent) with checkpoints.

## Next action

Implement dispatcher + optimized PELT arrays; do not ship the
current FPOP as a production engine.
