of \(D=B+L+\varepsilon\) against flat controls, without freezing

## Verdict

**PATH_B_CONDITIONAL_PASS.**

B_hierarchy is a defensible research prototype: it is the only
leakage-controlled candidate with nonzero hierarchical nested
recovery. Same-direction nested recovery is **not** yet
substantial enough to freeze as production architecture.

Do **not**:
- replace production PELT with B silently;
- claim region-level FDR;
- claim superiority to csaw/THOR;
- promote C or D.

Do:
- keep B as the Path B reference implementation;
- keep flat PELT as broad default / control;
- treat residual additive A as a negative control for nested;
- require a follow-up focused on same-direction nested
  identifiability before any production freeze.
