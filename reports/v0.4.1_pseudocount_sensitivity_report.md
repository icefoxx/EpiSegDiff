# v0.4.1 — Pseudocount sensitivity

## Objective

Quantify stability of effects and calls at 200 bp for
`log2(x + c)` with `c ∈ {0.001, 0.01, 0.05, 0.1}`. Reference `c = 0.01`.

## Methods

Same coarsened 200 bp linear matrix and `absolute 0.6` filter.
Bin-level effects were also stratified by quartiles of per-bin max
linear signal.

## Parameters

Penalty 24, `min_bins = 3`, FDR 0.05, contrast `JNKi - DMSO`.

## Results

FACT: all four values completed.

| c | tested | GAIN | LOSS | median \|effect\| | runtime_s |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.001 | 13028 | 2 | 668 | 1.037 | 58 |
| 0.01 | 13027 | 2 | 688 | 1.018 | 56 |
| 0.05 | 13026 | 2 | 787 | 0.942 | 55 |
| 0.1 | 13026 | 2 | 854 | 0.860 | 56 |

Concordance vs `c = 0.01` (all overlapping tested segments):

| c | Pearson | Spearman | sign concordance | DER bp-Jaccard | strong recovery |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.001 | 0.9995 | 0.9998 | 0.9997 | 0.970 | 0.976 |
| 0.05 | 0.9983 | 0.9990 | 0.9993 | 0.877 | 1.000 |
| 0.1 | 0.9953 | 0.9968 | 0.9986 | 0.809 | 1.000 |

Bin-level sign-flip rate vs 0.01, by linear-signal quartile:

| c | Q1 (lowest) | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 (highest) |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 0.001 | 0.0060 | 0.0132 | 0.0148 | 0.0158 |
| 0.05 | 0.0146 | 0.0317 | 0.0370 | 0.0371 |
| 0.1 | 0.0214 | 0.0482 | 0.0543 | 0.0565 |

Pearson in every quartile is ≥ 0.961.

FACT: 0.01 was the frozen v0.4 value. It was **not** chosen because
it maximizes DER count (0.1 yields more LOSS: 854 vs 688).

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: Q1 flip rates are **lower** than Q4 because Q1 is dominated
by near-zero bins whose condition means are both `log2(c)`, so the
effect is 0 for every `c`. This is **not** evidence that sensitivity
is concentrated in low-signal regions. Mid/high quartiles, where
`x` is comparable to `c`, move more.

INFERENCE: larger `c` shrinks `|effect|` (median 1.04 → 0.86) and
increases LOSS calls. The sign of the experiment (LOSS dominance)
does not reverse.

## Failures

None.

## Interpretation

At fixed 200 bp, effects are highly correlated across this `c` range.
Called-set Jaccard declines as `c` increases because more borderline
LOSS regions pass FDR. Strong-reference recovery stays ≥ 0.976.

## Limitations

Quartiles use per-bin max linear signal, not region means. Only the
GSE280947 JUN BigWigs were tested.

## Decision

Gate C: **PASS**.

## Next action

Treat pseudocount as **dataset-dependent**. Do not promote 0.01 to a
universal BigWig default.
