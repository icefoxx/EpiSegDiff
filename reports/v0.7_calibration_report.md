# v0.7 — Calibration and inference

## Objective

Compare same-data, pooled-geometry, sample-split, and cross-fit
inference, and repeat nulls under global, low-signal, structured,
and AR(1) backgrounds.

## Methods

**I1–I4** (`nrep=50`, 400-bin null, engines PELT/WBS/fused):
`run_multiscale` / `run_crossfit` with `infer_regions` (limma
when 2+2; descriptive `|Δ|≥0.5` if unreplicated).

**Extra nulls** (`nrep=50`): MAD-threshold detection, same
threshold as the factorial (not FDR).

Power: 10-bin Δ=1.5 event. WBS power used I1–I3. PELT/fused
power used MAD-threshold.

## Parameters

Penalty 8. Split = even samples segment, odd samples test.
Cross-fit = union of A→B and B→A.

## Results

FACT (`infer_regions` global null, P(any DER)):

| inference | pelt | fused | wbs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| same_data | 0.06 | 0.06 | 0.06 |
| pooled | 0.08 | 0.08 | 0.08 |
| split | **0.00** | **0.00** | **0.00** |
| crossfit | — | — | **0.00** |

FACT: MAD-threshold nulls (global / low-signal / structured /
AR(1)): **P(any DER) = 0** for PELT, fused, and WBS.

FACT: WBS power (10-bin event) vs P(any false): same-data
recall 0.12 / 0.06; pooled 0.12 / 0.08; split 0.02 / 0.00.

FACT: MAD-threshold power on the same 10-bin event: PELT
recall **1.00** / P(any false) **0**; fused 0.22 / 0; WBS 0 / 0.

FACT: v0.6 unfiltered same-data PELT P(any DER) was **0.36**
per 200 kb. v0.7 same-data infer_regions is **0.06** per 80 kb
and is not a like-for-like FDR claim.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: split and cross-fit improve calibration relative to
same-data, with a large power cost on the weak WBS cell.

INFERENCE: MAD-threshold calibration is acceptable on these
toys and is **not** a region-level FDR guarantee.

INFERENCE: pooled-signal geometry did **not** improve
calibration (0.08 vs 0.06).

UNRESOLVED: empirical FDP at nominal 0.01 / 0.05 / 0.10 for
every production candidate under limma FDR was not re-grid
beyond v0.6.

## Failures

Permutation of the full v0.7 engine suite on real data was not
re-run (v0.5/v0.6: 688 LOSS → 0/0 under the two balanced
label swaps). No p-value histogram for v0.7 infer_regions.

## Interpretation

Gate G: MAD-threshold candidates are not anti-conservative on
the four synthetic nulls. Same-data limma still has a small
nonzero P(any). Do **not** claim calibrated FDR.

Gate H: split/cross-fit reduce false calls vs same-data, with
unacceptable WBS power. PELT MAD-threshold already has power 1
and P(any)=0 on this design, so split is not required for that
cell.

## Decision

Production inference remains same-data limma on filtered real
profiles, with the v0.6 permutation caveat. Split is available
as a diagnostic, not the default.

## Next action

Do not advertise FDR control from this report.
