# v0.8 — Held-out benchmark

## Methods

Seeds 200000+ (widths) and 210000+ (nested); nrep=50. No
retuning after seeing these seeds.

## Results

FACT: B held-out F1 — 200 bp 0.70; 500 bp 0.18; ≥2 kb ≥0.93.
Matches fused on narrow; matches PELT on broad.

FACT: held-out nested `p_both` — B 0.10, C 0.30, A/flat 0.

FACT: benefits that existed on development F3 (B p_both 0.16)
persist directionally on held-out (0.10), without inflation.

## Decision

Gate I: **PASS** for generalization of B’s profile; nested
gain remains small.
