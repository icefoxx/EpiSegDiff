# v0.5 — Ablation

## Objective

Separate adaptive segmentation from “just using BigWig + limma”.

## Methods

Same matrix, transform, contrast:

1. PELT (primary ESD)
2. per-valid-bin limma, FDR≤0.05, unmerged
3. IDR-union peak limma

## Parameters

absolute 0.6 defines valid bins for (1) and (2).

## Results

FACT:

| ablation | GAIN | LOSS | units | median width |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| adaptive_PELT | 2 | 688 | 13,027 | 800 |
| fixed_bins_unmerged | 2 | 240 | 91,390 | 200 |
| fixed_peaks | 2 | 1,688 | 5,398 | 620 |

INFERENCE: PELT is not the same as calling bins. It recovers **more
LOSS** than per-bin FDR (688 vs 240) at 4× width. Peak regions remain
the most sensitive universe.

## Uncertainty

Unmerged bins under-count domain-level events (predeclared). Merged
bins were not the primary ablation table.

## Failures

None.

## Interpretation

Gate G: **PASS**. Segmentation contributes beyond raw bin tests.
It is not a substitute for asking whether peaks are a better
region universe.

## Decision

PASS.
