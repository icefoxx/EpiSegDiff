Explain same-direction nested absorption/misses in
`B_hierarchy`, and test whether recovery can improve without
breaking leakage/null/broad integrity.

## Why not PASS

No leakage-safe configuration improved same-direction `p_both`
over the v0.8 reference on the predeclared selection cell, so
there is no held-out *improvement* to claim. Weak/borderline/
high-noise held-out cells remain at `p_both=0`.

## Why not FAIL

The failure mechanism is characterized; an empirical
IDENTIFIABLE regime exists (large L / high residual SNR / low
noise); leakage and null guardrails hold for the reference;
broad integrity is preserved; BigWig path untouched.

## Answers to required questions

1. **Where do children disappear?** After residual construction,
   at child segmentation/thresholding (thin children). For fat
   children, during parent mean-subtraction (absorption→1).
2. **How much absorbed?** ≈2–3% for 400 bp/20 kb; →100% when
   width ratio is large.
3. **Absorption ↑ with B?** Weakly / no for thin children.
4. **↓ with L?** No meaningful dependence for thin children.
5. **Width ratio predicts absorption?** **Yes.**
6. **Boundary proximity?** Not the dominant predictor; center
   can be worse than near-edge in this grid.
7. **Finer resolution?** Helps moderately (0.12→0.32).
8. **Best predictors?** Residual SNR and L > L/B > B;
   absorption not predictive for thin children.
9. **Replicate/noise variance?** Yes — noise 0.1→0.4 collapses
   L=1.5 p_both from 0.92→0.04.
10. **Candidates generated then removed?** Liberal thr=0 overlap
    is almost always true; operational high-mean children pass
    or fail together with final `p_both`.
11. **Reconciliation reduce p_both?** Default has none; optional
    reconcile **hurts**.
12. **Parent change without damage?** Median/trim did not unlock
    gains; not promoted.
13. **Best child detector?** Fused ref ≥ thr0.25 (same p_both,
    worse false_child); PELT child useless here.
14. **p_both↑ with leak controlled?** **Not achieved** among
    tested controls.
15. **Pareto?** Ref is on the leakage-safe frontier; thr0.25
    same p_both, higher false_child.
16–17. **Broad-only / hetero?** Controlled for ref.
18. **Near boundaries?** Recoverable (held-out 0.62).
19. **Child widths?** Mixed; very wide children absorb.
20. **Moderate/high noise?** Gain does not persist.
21. **Held-out improvement?** None (chosen=ref).
22. **Stable identifiable regime?** Yes at high L / low noise.
23. **Ambiguous abstention?** Yes — recommend `AMBIGUOUS_CHILD`
    below IDENTIFIABLE.
24. **\|L\|≈1.2–1.8 universal?** **No.**
25. **Better descriptor?** Residual SNR / L-vs-noise / width
    ratio.
26. **B_hierarchy still best leakage-safe Path B ref?** **Yes.**
27. **Worth further development?** Yes, but **not by MAD-threshold
    retuning alone** — need a child isolator that finds high-mean
    local residual pieces, and/or non-mean parent orthogonalization
    for fat children.
28. **Next step?** **Neither production freeze nor abandonment.**
    Next authorized work should be a targeted child-isolation /
    orthogonalization redesign under the same leakage guardrail —
    still not v0.9 architecture expansion without review.
