# v0.8.1 — Empirical identifiability surface

## Frozen regime labels (before held-out)

```text
IDENTIFIABLE: p_both ≥ 0.8 and leak ≤ 0.05
AMBIGUOUS: 0.2 < p_both < 0.8 (leak OK)
NOT_RELIABLY_IDENTIFIABLE: p_both ≤ 0.2 or leak > 0.05
```

## Results

FACT (ref, same-direction BxL surface): 9 IDENTIFIABLE, 9
AMBIGUOUS, 35 NOT_RELIABLY_IDENTIFIABLE cells.

FACT: IDENTIFIABLE cells concentrate at **large L** (≳2.5 at
noise 0.2) / high residual SNR. Example: L=2.5 → mean p_both
0.81; L=3.0 → 0.93.

FACT: better predictors than absolute B: **L** and
**residual SNR**. L/B helps secondarily. Absorption does not
predict thin-child success.

FACT: noise shifts the boundary hard — at L=1.5, p_both is
0.92 / 0.14 / 0.04 for noise 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.4.

FACT: finer resolution helps moderately (50 bp 0.32 vs 200 bp
0.12 at B=1.25,L=1.5,noise=0.2).

## Interpretation

INFERENCE: `|L|≈1.2–1.8` is **not** a universal threshold. It
is an SNR/noise/width-ratio phenomenon. Prefer residual SNR /
L-vs-noise descriptions.

SPECULATION: an `AMBIGUOUS_CHILD` output is appropriate below
the IDENTIFIABLE regime rather than forcing children.

## Decision

Stable identifiable regime exists at high L / low noise; the
v0.8 operating point (L≈1.5, noise 0.2) is AMBIGUOUS/NOT.
