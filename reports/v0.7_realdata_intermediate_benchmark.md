# v0.7 — Real-data intermediate (GSE110378 ATAC)

## Objective

Run an accessibility-like assay through the same continuous
architecture.

## Methods

BigWigs GSM3400927/28/30/31. **Chromosome-restricted:** chr1
+ chr19, 500 bp mean summary, quantile 0.75 filter, contrast
KO − Control. PELT min3 penalty 24; fused lasso on the same
matrix.

Cache: `cache/gse110378_atac_500bp_chr1_chr19.rds`.

## Parameters

No predeclared ATAC direction of the same strength as
H3K27me3 GAIN. Kdm6b-KO can change accessibility locally.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: the continuous architecture is runnable on ATAC
BigWigs. That is a software/geometry result, not a biological
validation of the 48 GAIN calls.

UNRESOLVED: genome-wide ATAC, published peak set, and motif
support were not generated.

SPECULATION: 500 bp is a reasonable intermediate scale; 50–200
bp ATAC structure is not resolved here.

## Failures

Not genome-wide. No conventional ATAC peak comparator. No
direction lock.

## Interpretation

Gate / H6 intermediate: **partial PASS** (runs, produces
finite GAIN/LOSS, no crash). Not a scientific ATAC paper
result.

## Decision

Record ATAC as supported input, chromosome-restricted.

## Next action
