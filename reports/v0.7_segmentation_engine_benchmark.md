# v0.7 — Segmentation-engine benchmark

## Objective

Compare change-point engines on `raw_effect` with `nrep=50`.

## Methods

Engines: Gaussian PELT, binary segmentation, WBS, fused lasso
(1-D TV denoise + jump cps), 3-state HMM, HSMM (HMM + duration
merge). FPOP is implemented via `segment_effect` and was not
re-run as a primary cell (v0.6: cost mismatch on real z).

`min_width=1` for fused/WBS/binseg; `min_width=3` for
PELT/HMM/HSMM.

## Parameters

Same planted-block design as the score grid. Penalty 8.

## Results

FACT (mean F1, zeros included):

| engine | 200 bp | 500 bp | 2 kb | 10 kb | 50 kb |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| fused_lasso | **0.62** | 0.06 | 0.33 | 0.52 | 0.64 |
| hmm | 0.08 | **0.15** | 0.96 | 0.99 | 1.00 |
| hsmm | 0.08 | 0.12 | 0.94 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| pelt | 0.00 | 0.00 | **1.00** | **1.00** | **1.00** |
| wbs | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| binseg | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.02 | 1.00 |

FACT: fused lasso is the only engine with material 200 bp
recovery on `raw_effect`. It is **not** a universal winner: F1
collapses at 500 bp (0.06) and stays below PELT at every width
≥2 kb. Detected fused calls at 200 bp have median boundary error
0 (when they fire).

FACT: this WBS implementation (random-interval CUSUM, M=200,
penalty 8) does **not** recover 200 bp–2 kb events.

FACT: PELT retains perfect block recovery at 2–50 kb.

FACT: HMM/HSMM are intermediate: weak at 200–500 bp, nearly
perfect at ≥2 kb, slightly more boundary error than PELT.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: fused lasso helps because TV does not impose a
`min_bins` floor. It does not automatically preserve long
plateaus under the frozen λ = 2·MAD(diff).

INFERENCE: WBS failure here is an implementation/penalty
result, not a proof that WBS as a class is useless.

## Failures

WBS narrow: F1=0. Binseg ≤2 kb: F1=0. FPOP not in the primary
table. Exploratory effect×noise (n=10): fused F1 at 500 bp is
0.9 when Δ=1.5 and noise=0.1, 0.1 at noise=0.2, 0 at noise=0.4
— the 500 bp fused collapse is noise-sensitive.

## Interpretation

No single engine is valid in every regime. PELT is the broad
default. Fused lasso is a narrow-sensitive option, not a
replacement.

## Decision

Reject universal fused-lasso or universal WBS. Keep PELT as the
broad engine.
