# v0.7 — Score-engine benchmark

## Objective

Compare differential statistics on the same PELT geometry, with
`nrep=50` at primary widths `{200, 500, 2000, 10000, 50000}` bp
and `min_width ∈ {1, 3}`.

## Methods

Score engines: `raw_effect` (mean T − mean C), `log_effect`
(`log2(x+0.01)`), `standardized`
(`Δ / sqrt(s²_pooled + MAD(Δ)²)`), `hotelling` (signed √T²).
`moderated_t` and `signed_pval` are implemented but were not in
the primary 50-rep grid (they require per-bin limma).

Detection used a frozen MAD threshold on the segmented score,
not limma FDR.

## Parameters

Bin 200 bp. Noise 0.2. Δ = 1.5. Penalty 8. Seeds `70000+width+…`.

## Results

FACT: `raw_effect` + PELT mean F1 = **0** at 200 and 500 bp
(0 calls in 50/50 reps) and **1.00** at 2–50 kb (exactly one
call, zero boundary error).

FACT: `standardized` + PELT `min_width=1` mean F1 = **0.47**
(200 bp) and **0.64** (500 bp). That is the only score that
materially recovers the v0.6 narrow failure on PELT.

FACT: `hotelling` and `log_effect` detect almost every 200–500 bp
event but with tens of extra calls (precision 0.005–0.018). They
are not production candidates.

FACT: at ≥2 kb, `raw_effect` dominates (F1=1, n_call=1).
`standardized` fragments (mean 5.5 / 16.9 / 38 calls at 2 / 10 /
50 kb).

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: PELT’s 200–500 bp failure on `raw_effect` is a
penalty/min-width geometry problem, not an absence of signal.
Rescaling by a global MAD floor (`standardized`) lets one- and
two-bin spikes exceed the call threshold, at the cost of
broad-domain fragmentation.

SPECULATION: `hotelling` without a MAD floor over-calls because
tiny `s²_pooled` inflates T². That is the same failure mode as
an unfloored z-score.

## Failures

`moderated_t` / `signed_pval` were not scored in the n=50 grid
(computational). Exploratory only.

## Interpretation

Best **narrow** score (on PELT): `standardized`.
Best **broad** score: `raw_effect`.
Do not use one score for both regimes.

## Decision

Retain both scores. Do not replace production `raw_effect` with
`standardized` globally.
