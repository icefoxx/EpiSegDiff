# v0.2.1 — Extended null / near-null calibration (Task D)

**Objective.** Measure adaptive discovery + same-data BH testing under
strict null, near-null, spatially heterogeneous backgrounds, and a
sample-split comparator. Zero discoveries are *not* treated as proof of
FDR control.

**Hypothesis.** After TMM + \(Z\)-segmentation + edgeR QL + BH, the
probability of any false region at nominal \(q=0.05\) should be near
0.05 on a short strict-null chromosome. Weak structured effects may be
aggregated into calls. Same-data testing may be slightly
anti-conservative relative to sample splitting.

**Methods.** `run_simulated_discovery()` (TMM, PELT \(\lambda=10\)).
`empirical_fdr()` on each replicate. Binomial CIs via `binom.test`.
Sample split: discover on half the replicates, test on the rest
(`sample_split_discovery()`).

**Simulation design.**

| Family | Design | Independent genomes |
|--------|--------|---------------------|
| strict null | 3 regimes × 200 | ~193–200 each (600 at three \(q\)) |
| near-null | log2FC 0.1, 0.2, 0.3; one 25-bin block | 80 planned / ~75–77 used |
| heterogeneous null | drift, high-bg block, coverage wave, mask | 60 planned; mask under-ran |
| selection | 4 vs 4 null, same-data vs split | 40 planned |

`n_bins = 250`. Config: `config/validation/null_calibration.yaml`.
Table: `benchmark/simulation/null_calibration.tsv` (2810 rows).

**Parameters.** Seed 20263. Nominal \(q \in \{0.01, 0.05, 0.10\}\).

**Results.**

FACT: Strict-null probability of **any** discovery:

| \(q\) | \(\hat p(\mathrm{any})\) | 95% CI | n rows |
|------|--------------------------|--------|--------|
| 0.01 | 0.018 | — | 600 |
| 0.05 | 0.052 | 0.035–0.073 | 577 |
| 0.10 | 0.092 | — | 600 |

FACT: At \(q=0.05\), mean false-region count = 0.061 (median 0). Mean
false-bp ≈ 1.7 kb on a 50 kb toy chromosome.

FACT: Near-null call rate at \(q=0.05\): 2.6% (0.1), 13% (0.2), 69%
(0.3). Mean \(n\) discoveries at 0.3 is 1.13.

FACT: Same-data 4-rep null: \(\hat p(\mathrm{any}, q=0.05)=0.13\)
(CI 0.043–0.27, n=39). Sample-split: **0 / 37** (CI 0–0.095).

FACT: Heterogeneous-null pooled \(\hat p=0.13\) (n=47). Mask subset is
n=4 and is not interpreted.

INFERENCE: On the primary 3-regime strict null the procedure is
**approximately calibrated**, not grossly anti-conservative and not
vacuously conservative. Weak effects of 0.3 log2 are often aggregated
into a called region. Sample splitting is more conservative than
same-data testing; this is a measurement, not a default replacement.

UNRESOLVED: Genome-scale (millions of bins) calibration. Heterogeneous
and 4-rep same-data cells are too small for a firm anti-conservative
claim.

**Uncertainty.** Strict-null CIs are reported above. Heterogeneous and
split CIs are wide.

**Failures.** Some simulations failed to bind in an earlier draft
(fixed). Mask heterogeneous arm under-ran. Failed/skipped genomes were
not silently deleted from the row count we could keep; under-run arms
are stated.

**Interpretation.** BH after adaptive discovery is **not** claimed as
selective FDR. Empirically, on this design, nominal 5% is close to a 5%
any-discovery rate.

**Limitations.** 250 bins. One penalty. TMM only.

**Decision: CONDITIONAL PASS (Gate D)**

Hundreds of strict-null genomes, CIs, near-null, and a selection
experiment are in hand. Heterogeneous-background evidence is thin.

**Next action.** Task E integrated event-class benchmark. Do not claim
genome-wide FDR.
