# v0.2.1 — Final acceptance report

**Objective.** Decide whether EpiSegDiff may proceed to real-data
benchmarking after the statistical-validation release. No NB-FPOP. No
competitor benchmarking in this phase.

**Hypothesis.** The v0.2 risks (composition flanks, overlapping
topology, uncalibrated nulls, peak-dependent boundaries) can be
measured and, where possible, reduced without a new likelihood engine.

**Methods.** Tasks A–E in the required order. Reports A–D were written
before Task E.

**Simulation design / Parameters.** See the five preceding v0.2.1
reports and `config/validation/`.

**Results — answers required by the task.**

1. **Which normalization should be default, and under what assumptions?**  
   FACT/INFERENCE: **TMM** remains the default when the differential
   fraction and GAIN:LOSS mix are unknown. It keeps
   \(|\mathrm{bias}_{\mathrm{background}}| \lesssim 0.08\) at 10%
   one-sided GAIN and stays centered under balanced 50:50 shifts even
   at 50% of the genome. Use **`background`** (lower-half
   median-of-ratios) when a *large one-sided* composition shift is
   expected.

2. **Where does it fail?**  
   FACT: TMM fails (bias −0.25 to −0.83) at 25–50% **one-sided** GAIN.
   `background` fails (bias +0.23 to +0.61) at 25–50% **balanced**
   GAIN:LOSS. `library_size` fails from 5% one-sided GAIN (bias −0.16,
   extra false flanks).

3. **Is background \(\hat\beta\) acceptably centered?**  
   FACT: Yes at 0% for all methods, and for TMM up to ~10% one-sided or
   large balanced shifts. No for library-size once composition moves.

4. **Does segmentation create systematic false flanks?**  
   FACT: Yes under library-size on one-sided GAIN (median 9 false
   regions at 25% vs 0.5 for `background`). Reduced but not zero under
   TMM at large one-sided fractions.

5. **Median and 90th-percentile boundary errors?**  
   FACT (Task C, `end_walk`, no peak caller): narrow — left median/p90
   350/350 bp, right 0/400 bp. Broad — left 7800/7800 bp, right 0/8000
   bp. Task E matched MAE is 0 on bin-aligned GAIN/LOSS.

6. **Are EXPANSION, CONTRACTION, LEFT_SHIFT, RIGHT_SHIFT distinguishable?**  
   FACT: Yes **by definition** (Task B partition, 200/200 each).
   **Not reliably** when domains are inferred from condition-mean
   quantiles on NB counts (Task E).

7. **Was `BIDIRECTIONAL_SHIFT` removed or redefined?**  
   FACT: **Removed** as a primary class. Opposite-end motion is
   EXPANSION, CONTRACTION, or COMPLEX.

8. **How often is COMPLEX returned?**  
   FACT: 6.7% of Task B predictions; 28% of Task E TMM pipeline event
   tables. COMPLEX remains the residual, not a forced named class.

9. **Does topology require external peak boundaries?**  
   FACT: **No.** Discovery is \(\hat\beta\rightarrow\) segmentation.
   Domains and `end_walk` use counts only. `peak_caller = none` on
   validation rows.

10. **Strict-null, near-null, heterogeneous?**  
    FACT: Strict-null \(P(\mathrm{any\ call}\mid q=0.05)=0.052\)
    (CI 0.035–0.073) on ~577 genomes. Near-null: 2.6% / 13% / 69% at
    log2FC 0.1 / 0.2 / 0.3. Heterogeneous evidence is thin (n=47).

11. **Same-data discovery/testing calibration?**  
    FACT: Approximately calibrated on the primary strict-null grid.
    On a small 4-rep same-data subset, \(P(\mathrm{any})=0.13\);
    sample-split was 0/37. **INFERENCE:** not grossly
    anti-conservative; not a selective-FDR proof.

12. **Ready for real-data benchmarking?**  
    **CONDITIONAL PASS:** ready for an **explicitly scoped real-data
    pilot** — one paired-end CUT&RUN/ChIP narrow mark, TMM default,
    Level I (GAIN/LOSS) as the primary output, Level II experimental.
    **Not** ready for a full narrow+broad+competitor campaign.

13. **What would justify future NB-FPOP?**  
    UNRESOLVED / candidate problems, not a mandate:
    - broad-domain left-boundary MAE of several kb under local
      Gaussian `end_walk`;
    - residual false flanks under TMM at large one-sided shifts
      (normalization, not necessarily NB cost);
    - pipeline topology from noisy condition means.
    None of these yet specify an NB segment cost that would fix them.
    Phase 14 remains blocked.

**Uncertainty.** Short chromosomes. 4–5 Monte Carlo seeds on stress
and integrated grids. Heterogeneous-null n is small.

**Failures.** YAML `NULL` class dropped from Task E; 2 stress-test
cells errored (kept). Broad-domain boundaries not recovered.

**Interpretation.** The main v0.2 statistical risks are now *measured*.
Composition bias has a documented envelope. Topology rules no longer
overlap. Null calibration is no longer “zero calls = success.”

**Limitations.** No real BAM. No competitors (prohibited here). No
NB-FPOP (prohibited).

**Decision: CONDITIONAL PASS**

Proceed only to a scoped real-data pilot (one narrow count assay,
TMM, Level I primary). Do not open full competitor benchmarking or
NB-FPOP until that pilot is logged.

**Next action.**

1. One real paired-end CUT&RUN or ChIP-seq cohort (narrow mark).
2. Record TMM vs `background` sensitivity on that cohort.
3. Keep competitor runs in the log when that pilot is done — not before.
