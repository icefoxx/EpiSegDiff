# v0.6 — Dataset and comparator selection

## Objective

Freeze datasets and comparators **before** seeing v0.6 synthetic or
calibration outcomes. Selection is not based on favorable EpiSegDiff
rankings.

## Comparators

| Method | Status | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |

| **THOR** | **NOT_AVAILABLE** | `rgt-THOR` binary absent; no BAMs |

| PePr | **NOT_RUN** | optional |

## Decision

Primary isolation of **region definition** uses BigWig+limma for
ESD, fixed peaks, and merged significant windows.

End-to-end csaw/THOR on real BAM is recorded NOT_AVAILABLE, not
faked by sending BigWig values to edgeR.
