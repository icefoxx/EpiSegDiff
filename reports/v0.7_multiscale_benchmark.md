# v0.7 — Multi-scale benchmark

## Objective

Test single-scale, coarse-to-fine (×5 + edge CUSUM), and
candidate-union (fused ∪ PELT) strategies.

## Methods

`nrep=50` at the primary widths. Union resolves overlaps by
larger |effect|. Nested geometry: 100-bin broad + 2-bin spike
inside (50 reps). Routing uses no truth.

## Parameters

Penalty 8. Coarse factor 5. Nested seed `72000+rep`.

## Results

FACT (mean F1):

| width | single | coarse-to-fine | union fused∪PELT |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 200 (fused) | 0.60 | 0.03 | 0.60 |
| 500 (fused) | 0.10 | 0.46 | 0.10 |
| 2000 (PELT) | 1.00 | **0.00** | 1.00 |
| 10 kb (PELT) | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |
| 50 kb (PELT) | 1.00 | 1.00 | 1.00 |

FACT: candidate union matches the better single-scale engine at
each width and does **not** destroy 2–50 kb PELT performance.

FACT: coarse-to-fine **hurts** 200 bp (0.03 vs 0.60) and
**eliminates** 2 kb PELT detections (0 vs 1). It helps only the
500 bp fused cell (0.46 vs 0.10).

FACT (nested): every engine, including `union_wbs_pelt`, has
mean `n_merged = 1`. The broad and narrow components are
recovered as **one** interval. PELT/union F1 ≈ 0.96 by covering
the union of the two truths; fused F1 0.82 with extra splits.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: union is a safe aggregator, not a true multi-scale
decomposer. It does not separate nested scales.

INFERENCE: coarse-to-fine as implemented (mean of 5 bins, then
local CUSUM) is not a default.

UNRESOLVED: a duration-aware HSMM or explicit two-layer model
might separate nested events; the current HSMM did not, because
it was not run on the nested cell as a dedicated two-scale
output.

## Failures

Gate F (nested multi-scale representation) is **not met**.

## Interpretation

Multi-scale **candidate generation** is supported. Multi-scale
**event identity** (narrow-on-broad as two objects) is not.

## Decision

Production may use union as a conservative ensemble. Do not
ship coarse-to-fine as default.

## Next action

Do not claim genuine nested-scale recovery.
