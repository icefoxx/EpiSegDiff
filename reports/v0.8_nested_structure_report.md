# v0.8 — Nested structure

## Objective

Measure parent/child recovery without summarizing by union F1.

## Methods

F3/F4 (n=50) and full B×L matrix (B,L ∈ {0.6,1.2,1.8}×±,
nrep=50). Metric `p_both` = scale-typed parent and child both
hit; `p_merged` = parent without child (absorption).

## Results

FACT: flat controls always `p_both=0`, `p_merged=1` when they
cover the nested pair.

FACT: **B_hierarchy** F3 same-dir `p_both=0.16`, F4 opp
`p_both=0.40`. Held-out nested `p_both=0.10`.

FACT: B×L same-direction for B — `p_both` rises with |L|
(0.04 at L=0.6 → ~0.40 at L=1.8) and is only weakly sensitive
to B. Nested same-direction remains mostly absorbed when the
local boost is weak.

FACT: **C** F4 `p_both=0.84` but fails leakage elsewhere.

FACT: **A** `p_both=0` — sequential residualization absorbs
locals into B.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: hierarchy helps when the local residual is large
(especially reversals). Same-direction enhancement is still
mostly a formulation hard case.

## Decision

Gate D: **PARTIAL** — directional improvement over flat, not
substantial on same-direction primary cells.
