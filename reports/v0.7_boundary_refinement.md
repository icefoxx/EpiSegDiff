# v0.7 — Boundary refinement

## Objective

Measure boundary error by engine, width, and sharp vs gradual
shape, and test optional local CUSUM edge refinement.

## Methods

Primary grid records `med_bound_err` when a call overlaps truth.
Gradual vs sharp: 25-bin (5 kb) event, `nrep=50`, engines PELT /
WBS / fused, `min_width=1`. `refine_segment_edges` is a local
CUSUM snap; `refine_boundaries` (v0.2 BED+track API) is
unchanged.

## Parameters

Penalty 8. Gradual shape: 20% linear ramps at each end.

## Results

FACT (detected calls only): PELT at 2–50 kb has mean |boundary
error| **0 bp**. HMM/HSMM: 72–172 bp at 2–10 kb. Fused lasso:
0 at 200 bp when it fires; **21.7 kb** at 10 kb and **81 kb**
at 50 kb (over-wide plateaus).

FACT (5 kb sharp vs gradual, mean F1 / med bound err):

| engine | sharp F1 | sharp err | gradual F1 | gradual err |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| pelt | 1.00 | 0 | 0.89 | 1008 bp |
| fused_lasso | 0.66 | 10264 | 0.44 | 15280 |
| wbs | 0.50 | 17387 | (no gradual rows) | |

FACT: WBS gradual cell produced no tabulated gradual rows in
the aggregate that included `med_bound_err` (detections too
sparse or NA error).

FACT: merge/split on two 10-bin same-direction events:

| engine | gap 0 n_merged | gap 2 | gap 10 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| pelt | 1.00 | 1.00 | 0.90 |
| fused_lasso | 0.78 | 0.22 | 0.12 |
| wbs | 0.00 (no calls) | 0.00 | 0.00 |

PELT merges adjacent same-direction events. Fused merges less
as the gap grows. WBS simply fails to call.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: PELT’s zero boundary error on sharp blocks is the
easy piecewise-constant case. Gradual ramps cost ~1 kb, still
better than fused/WBS here.

SPECULATION: local CUSUM refinement cannot recover nested
narrow-on-broad identity; it only jogs an existing edge.

## Failures

No bootstrap/replicate boundary-confidence vs error calibration
table was produced. Do not expose a user-facing confidence
score.

## Interpretation

Gate D: PELT remains the accurate broad reconstructor. Fused
improves 200 bp detection but not 5–50 kb boundaries.

## Decision

Keep optional `refine_segment_edges`. Do not advertise
validated boundary confidence.
