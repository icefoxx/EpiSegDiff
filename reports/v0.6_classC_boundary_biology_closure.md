# v0.6 — Class-C and promoter-geometry closure

## Objective

Decompose v0.5 ESD-only regions (n=361) into C1–C4 and test
whether promoter enrichment is geometric.

## Methods

Predeclared priority C3>C2>C1>C4 against the **union IDR peak
set** (not only DERs). Motif: JUN/AP-1 as in v0.5. Promoter:
TxDb ±2 kb; Fisher OR vs A, width-matched A, and chrom+width
shuffle.

## Parameters

GSE280947 ESD DERs class C from v0.5 classification.

## Uncertainty

INFERENCE: most “ESD-only” intervals are **not** peak-boundary
extensions (C2 is 13%). They are **C4**, tens of kb from the
nearest IDR peak, promoter-enriched even after width/chrom
shuffle. Motif rate (34%) is only slightly above genomic
background (25%) and below shared DERs (67%).

SPECULATION: C4 may mix residual TSS-proximal JUN signal that
IDR missed and low-specificity promoter calls.

## Failures

Distance-to-peak–matched promoter control (plan item 2) was not
a separate successful matching beyond C4’s own distal distances.

## Interpretation

Gate H: **PASS**. Class C is mostly **peak-distal candidates**,
not mostly reconstruction of known peaks. Motif does **not**
elevate C4 to shared-DER quality.

Promoter enrichment is **not** a width artifact. It is
**unresolved** between biology and TSS-biased calling.

## Decision

Do not equate C4 with novel validated JUN biology.

## Next action

Orthogonal ATAC/RNA if obtained; do not retune classes.
